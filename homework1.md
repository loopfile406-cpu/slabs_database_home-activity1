Strategic Level (Top of Pyramid)

Information System: ESS (Executive Support System)

Data Flow & Purpose:
This level looks at the bigger picture of the business. It uses summarized information such as profits, company growth, and future opportunities. The information helps executives decide where the company shouldgo and what long-term goals must be achieved.

Tactical Level (Middle of Pyramid)

Information System: MIS/DSS (Management Information System / Decision Support System)

Data Flow & Purpose:
Managers use information that comes from daily business activities and turn it into reports. These reports help them plan work, solve problems, and make decisions for their departments. It focuses more on improving performance than doing day-to-day tasks.

Operational Level (Bottom of Pyramid)

Information System: TPS (Transaction Processing System)

Data Flow & Purpose:
This level handles everyday activities such as sales, customer records, or payments. The system collects detailed data and stores it accurately. That information becomes the foundation for the higher managementlevels.

Information Flow

Information starts at the operational level where daily transactions happen. It then moves to the tactical level where managers analyse it. Finally, the information reaches the strategic level where executives use it to make long-term business decisions.

Part 2: Technical Architecture Definitions
1. IT Infrastructure Diagram

An IT Infrastructure Diagram shows the physical technology a company owns and uses. This includes computers, servers, printers, network devices, and cables. It helps people understand what hardware is available and how everything is connected.

2. IT Landscape Diagram

An IT Landscape Diagram focuses more on systems and applications than physical equipment. Instead of showing actual devices, it groups technology into categories such as databases, business applications, and cloud services. It gives a high-level view of the company's technology environment.

3. IT Architecture Diagram

An IT Architecture Diagram shows the rules and structure that guide technology in the organisation. It explains how systems should work together and what standards must be followed. It helps ensure that future technology decisions are organised and consistent
